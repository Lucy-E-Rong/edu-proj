Cybersecurity Foundations Mock Textbook

Chapter 1: Threat Basics
Cybersecurity focuses on protecting confidentiality, integrity, and availability. Common threats include phishing, malware, credential theft, and insider abuse. Attackers often combine social engineering with technical exploits.

Chapter 2: Authentication and Access
Strong authentication combines passphrases and multi-factor authentication. Least privilege limits each account to the minimum permissions required. Access reviews should be scheduled to remove stale privileges.

Chapter 3: Network Defense
Segmentation divides networks into controlled zones. Firewalls and IDS/IPS systems enforce policy and detect suspicious behavior. Defense-in-depth assumes one control may fail and layers controls accordingly.

Chapter 4: Incident Response
A practical incident response process includes preparation, detection, containment, eradication, recovery, and lessons learned. Teams should preserve evidence and document timeline details during events.

Chapter 5: Secure Communication
Users should validate URLs before entering credentials and avoid unexpected attachments. Security awareness programs are more effective when exercises reflect realistic workplace scenarios.
