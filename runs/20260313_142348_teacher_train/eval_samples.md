## Output
What is the difference between symmetric and asymmetric encryption, and why is asymmetric encryption important for secure communication over the internet?

This question helps beginners understand the fundamental concepts of encryption, which is crucial for cybersecurity. Symmetric encryption uses the same key for encryption and decryption, while asymmetric encryption uses a pair of keys (public and private) for these processes. Asymmetric encryption is important for secure communication over the internet because it allows for secure key exchange and digital signatures, ensuring data integrity and confidentiality.
